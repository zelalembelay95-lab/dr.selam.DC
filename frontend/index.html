Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be registered to book?

No — fill in your details on the Book page and we'll register you automatically. You'll get a patient card number to use next time.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Just yourself and, if you have one, your patient card number. If you have relevant medical history, it helps to mention it when you arrive.

Can I cancel or change an appointment?

Yes. Log in with your patient card, open "My appointments," and cancel or book a new time.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes — call our emergency line any time and we'll do our best to fit you in the same day.
